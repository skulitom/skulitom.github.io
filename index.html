Learned control & simulation

Haltere

20 September 2026: a scripted figure-eight with yaw assistance, beside live neural activity. This clip shows the published motor controller.

A connectome-constrained recurrent network trained to fly a drone in Liftoff. Its 30,000-neuron flight circuit uses fruit-fly connectivity, with learned parameters and a differentiable PyTorch simulator.

As of 22 September 2026, an experimental controller with visual race-cue assistance has completed three-lap races on two seen courses.

Research prototype. These results use explicit pilot assistance; completion on unseen courses and freestyle flight remain unproven.

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • Differentiable simulation
