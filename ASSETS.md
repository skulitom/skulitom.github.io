These assets represent Artem's existing projects and published applications. Project and app imagery retains its original ownership; no general reuse licence is granted here.

- `assets/export-atlas.jpg`: actual browser capture of [Export Atlas](https://skulitom.github.io/export-atlas/) on 11 September 2026.
- `assets/london-map.jpg`: actual browser capture of [London in minutes](https://skulitom.github.io/london-time-map/) on 11 September 2026.
- `assets/litharness.webp`: existing [LitHarness banner artwork](https://github.com/skulitom/LitHarness/blob/main/docs/banner.png), converted to WebP. This is project artwork, not a screenshot of the application.
- `assets/cathode.webp`: actual [Cathode application screenshot](https://github.com/skulitom/CathodeDisplay/blob/main/docs/images/cathode-dark-arcade.jpg), converted to WebP.
- `assets/primordia-preview.mp4` and `assets/primordia-poster.webp`: the actual eight-second [Primordia simulation reel](https://github.com/skulitom/primordia/blob/main/docs/images/hero.webp), converted from animated WebP to H.264 MP4 without changing its frame timing. The poster is its first frame. The reel shows Physarum, Particle Life, Lenia, and reaction–diffusion. MIT notice: `assets/PRIMORDIA-LICENSE.txt`. The muted reel loops automatically while visible, with a discreet pause control. Reduced-motion preferences disable automatic playback; offscreen and background playback is paused.
- `assets/greek-icon.webp` and `assets/greek-screen.webp`: actual icon and screenshot from the [Greek Letters Quiz listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.greekletters.quiz).
- `assets/armour-icon.webp` and `assets/armour-screen.webp`: actual icon and screenshot from the [Medieval Armor Quiz listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.armourquiz.medieval).
- `assets/roman-icon.webp` and `assets/roman-screen.webp`: actual icon and screenshot from the [Roman Emperors Quiz listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.romanemperor.quiz).
- `assets/artificial-life.webp` and `assets/life-seed.bin`: a still and matching pre-grown state from the portfolio’s Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion simulation, generated by `scripts/generate_life.mjs`. The model adapts the reference reaction–diffusion world in Artem’s Primordia project (Coral Reef parameters). This is simulated chemistry, not observed biological data. Primordia’s MIT notice is retained in `assets/PRIMORDIA-LICENSE.txt`.

The Google Play images were retrieved on 11 September 2026 and converted to WebP for delivery. The portfolio shows each complete app screen at its original aspect ratio.

## 17 October 2026 Pixelbolt pixel art

The hero, the footer strip, and the Anode, Inkbolt, Cutbolt, Pixelbolt, and Nestbolt illustrations are original, decorative pixel art made with [Pixelbolt](https://github.com/skulitom/Pixelbolt) for this portfolio and the GitHub profile. Every pixel is a Pixelbolt render from editable recipes: the liquids and smoke are Pixelbolt fluid simulations (FLIP liquid and stable-fluid gas), shaded and framed with Pixelbolt's drawing operations in one shared palette. They depict each tool's idea, not application screenshots.

- `assets/pixel/hero/wide/` and `assets/pixel/hero/narrow/`: the hero, a night workbench, in two crops. `wide` is the desktop hero from x 182 (234 × 162: the window, the coil, the flask and the monitor); `narrow` is the phone composition from y 50 (156 × 130). Each holds a still background (`bg.png`), the steam and liquid as 96-frame sprite sheets with Pixelbolt atlases, the mostly still groups as `*-still.png` plus `*-moving.png` (the box that changes, one strip of 96 frames), and `still.png` (frame 50). All are cut from the piece's recipes and checked to recombine to the approved hero exactly. The `*.fluid.json` files are the piece's own fluid sources with the shared palette inlined; `hero.json` holds the layer positions, the vent, the bulb, the art's biggest billows, and the recipe operations the steam layer adds on top of the gas.
- `assets/pixel/footer/`: the footer strip under the same bench (416 × 42): its two still halves stacked as `bg.png`, a 48-frame Pixelbolt sheet for the dish and the drip, and `still.png` (frame 24).
- `assets/pixel/cards/<tool>/`: each card's Pixelbolt sheet and atlas (72-frame loop), its hover burst, and its still frame.
- `assets/pixelbolt/`: Pixelbolt's `fluid.js`, `core.js`, `authoring.js`, `craft.js`, `autotile.js`, `font.js`, and `runtime.js`, copied unmodified from the git objects of commit `671690f` (Pixelbolt main after #20, which added `createLiveFluid`; see `SOURCE.txt`), under Pixelbolt's MIT licence (`assets/pixelbolt/LICENSE`). The page uses `runtime.js` to draw the sheets, and `fluid.js` with its imports to run the hero's steam and flask live in the visitor's browser.

`assets/portfolio-social-pixel-20261017.png` is the 1280 × 640 link preview: the profile README's header still (`assets/v4/hero-still.svg` in the profile repository: this Pixelbolt hero with the name and tagline as vector text) rendered at 3× on `#0e1423`. The earlier share images remain for cached pages.

`scripts/import_art.mjs` refreshes the hero, footer and card art and the vendored modules from the art workspace. The earlier SVG illustrations (`assets/anode.svg`, `assets/*-20261017.svg`) and the artificial-life study (`life.js`, `assets/life-seed.bin`, `assets/artificial-life.webp`) remain available for cached pages.
